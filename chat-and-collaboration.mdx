# Chat and Collaboration

InvoiceHub includes a lightweight internal chat experience for team coordination.

## What you can do

- Open conversations with teammates
- Send invoice-related messages in context
- Receive updates as conversations change

## Why it helps

It reduces back-and-forth over email and keeps invoice questions inside the same workspace.
