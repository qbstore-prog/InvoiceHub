# FAQ

## Can users upload invoices?

Yes. Regular users can upload invoices and view their own invoice history.

## Can admins see everything?

Yes. Admins can view all team invoices, reports, and team settings.

## Does the app flag mismatches?

Yes. Expensar can flag invoices when the submitted NIF/VAT does not match the configured company identifier.
