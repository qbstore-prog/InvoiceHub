# invoiceHub Documentation

InvoiceHub is a modern invoice management platform designed for small businesses, finance teams, and growing organizations that need a simpler way to handle invoices. It brings invoice uploads, approvals, reporting, and team collaboration into one shared workspace so every invoice can be tracked from submission to review without relying on scattered emails or spreadsheets.

## What the app does

InvoiceHub helps teams:

- upload invoice files and receipts securely
- organize invoices by category, amount, date, and team member
- track expenses through dashboards and detailed reports
- flag NIF/VAT mismatches for review
- collaborate with teammates inside the same workspace

## Why teams use InvoiceHub

- Centralized invoice storage instead of scattered email attachments
- Better visibility for admins and finance leads
- Faster invoice capture with structured metadata
- Clear separation between admin and user workflows
- Built-in support for team onboarding and communication

## Quick start

1. Sign up or log in to the app.
2. Create or join a team.
3. Upload invoices from the Upload page.
4. Review expenses from the dashboard and reports pages.
5. Use the team tools to invite colleagues and coordinate follow-up.
