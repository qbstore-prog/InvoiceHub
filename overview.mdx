# Overview

Expensar is a collaborative invoice management platform for teams and small businesses that want a more organized and transparent way to manage spending. It gives admins a clear view of company expenses and workflows, while team members can upload invoices, add context, and follow progress in a shared system.

## Core capabilities

- secure invoice file upload for images and PDFs
- role-based workflows for admins and team members
- automatic invoice numbering and company-level settings
- NIF/VAT mismatch detection to reduce errors
- dashboards, reports, and team collaboration features

## Why it is useful

Expensar removes manual spreadsheet work and email-based invoice tracking. Instead, invoices are captured, categorized, and reviewed in a single place, making it easier to stay organized and audit spending.

## Main user roles

### Admin
An admin can:
- review all team invoices
- invite and remove team members
- configure company settings such as NIF/VAT and invoice numbering
- view dashboards and reporting summaries

### Team member
A team member can:
- upload invoices
- view their own invoice history
- collaborate with other members through the in-app chat
- follow invoices through the shared workspace
