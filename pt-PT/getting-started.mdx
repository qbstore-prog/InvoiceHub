# Getting Started

## 1. Create an account

Sign up at the login screen. New admins create the initial team automatically, while invited users join an existing team.

## 2. Configure the team

Admins can update the team name, company NIF/VAT number, and starting invoice number from the Settings page.

## 3. Invite teammates

Use the Team page to invite colleagues by email and manage members.

## 4. Upload invoices

Use the Upload page to add invoice files, purchase dates, categories, amount values, and optional notes.

## 5. Review reporting

Admins can inspect team spending from the dashboard and the reports section, while users can view their own invoice history from My Invoices.
