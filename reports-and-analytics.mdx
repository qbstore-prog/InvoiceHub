# Reports and Analytics

The dashboard and reports pages give admins an overview of the team’s invoice activity.

## Available insights

- Total invoices and total spend
- Current month spend
- NIF mismatch counts
- Category breakdown by amount
- Member breakdown by contribution
- Date-range filters

## Use cases

Use these views for month-end close, expense reviews, and budget oversight.
