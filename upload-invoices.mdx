# Upload Invoices

The Upload page is the main entry point for invoice submission. It is designed to make invoice capture simple, fast, and consistent for every team member.

## What you can do

- upload JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, or PDF files
- add purchase date, category, amount, and notes
- enter a NIF/VAT value when available
- preview image files before submission
- submit the file through secure cloud storage integration

## Why this matters

A well-structured upload process helps finance teams avoid missing information and makes later reporting more reliable. Consistent categories and dates improve spend visibility and simplify month-end review.

## Best practice

Use consistent categories such as Food, Utilities, Gas, or Road Payment so reporting stays clear and comparable.
